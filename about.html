About

Who I Am

I'm Jack Hendrickson — a musician, mixer, producer, and content creator based in Virginia. I've been playing guitar most of my life, and I do everything lefty in a world that largely builds gear for the other 90% of players. That's always been part of the drive.

My channel covers a lot of ground: metal and rock guitar covers, mix tutorials, gear reviews, and production breakdowns. I track, mix, and produce everything myself — using a setup built from years of obsessive gear research and a studio full of tools I actually trust.

What you see on YouTube is the tip of the iceberg. Behind every cover is a full session — tabs, stems, backing tracks, preset builds, and a mix I'm genuinely proud of. The Patreon and store are where all of that lives.

I'm also building something specific for the lefty guitar community — a corner of the internet that actually speaks to us. Gear that fits our hands, tabs that match what we see on screen, and tutorials that don't assume you'll just flip everything in your head.

If you make music, love gear, or you're just a fellow southpaw trying to figure it all out — you're in the right place.
