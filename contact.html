Work Together

Get in Touch

I'm open to brand partnerships, gear loans and reviews, affiliate collaborations, and content collabs with other creators. If your product is genuinely something I'd use — let's talk.

I'm also available to mix your songs — if you've tracked your guitars, bass, or full band and need someone to take it over the finish line, get in touch. And if you're a guitarist or musician looking to collaborate on a cover, I'm always down to work with other creators. Whether that's a full co-production or just trading parts remotely, reach out and let's make something.

jackhendricksonmusic@gmail.com

I'm particularly interested in:

  • Guitar and bass brands with lefty models
  • Plugin companies in the metal / rock space
  • Gear reviews for audio interfaces, mics, and studio equipment
  • Affiliate partnerships on products I already own and use
  • Mixing songs for independent artists and fellow creators
  • Cover collaborations with other guitarists and musicians on YouTube
